Player 1 · Harshitha

about-me.txt

Hi, I'm Harshitha Kalidindi.

Outside of work, I've recently been involved in conservation work, which has quietly reshaped how I see the world — teaching me to think beyond the present and pay attention to what truly sustains us. That awareness has made me more curious about people too — how we feel, how we make meaning, and what moves us.

Maybe that's why I'm drawn to psychology and to films that hold a single emotion so gently and completely. Somewhere between observing nature and understanding human emotion, I'm slowly growing into a storyteller, learning to move through the world with more clarity and intention.

That's a little about me. The rest, I'd rather share in a conversation.
